# Code of Conduct

## Our Standards

We want Amux to be a respectful, practical, and welcoming project for contributors and users.

Expected behavior:

- Be considerate and constructive.
- Assume good intent while still being clear about problems.
- Keep feedback focused on the work.
- Respect different backgrounds, experience levels, and viewpoints.

Unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, insults, threats, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Publishing private information without consent.
- Repeated disruption of project discussions.

## Scope

This code of conduct applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, releases, and community channels connected to Amux.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers through GitHub. Include relevant links, screenshots, and context when possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close issues, moderate discussions, block users, or take other reasonable action to keep the project healthy. Enforcement decisions are based on context, impact, and repeated behavior.

Participants are expected to cooperate with reasonable maintainer requests during review or enforcement.
